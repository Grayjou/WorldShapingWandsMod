[h1]World Shaping Wands[/h1]

[b]46 wands across 11 families[/b] for large-scale world editing, plus 2 specialized Torch Wheel wands. Build, shape, paint, wire, flood, torch, constrain, plan, and mold any region of your Terraria world using geometric shapes.

[h2]🔨 11 Wand Families[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Building[/b] — Place tiles, walls, platforms, rope, planter boxes, tracks, and grass seeds in bulk. Slope/HalfBlock application (6 slope types + actuation on/off/ignore). Block exhaustion modes (NextBlock, Interrupt, Cancel). Paint Sprayer integration (inventory or Coating wand paint). Vacuum item collection. Substrate-variant aware (skips grass/moss variants when replacing). Progressive batching for large operations.

[*][b]Dismantling[/b] — Mass-destroy tiles and walls within a shape. Validates pickaxe power. Container destruction with auto-unlock and item dropping. Void Everything mode. Delicate tile protection (Shadow Orbs, Plantera Bulbs, Bee Larvae, Life Fruit). Demon altar destruction protection toggle.

[*][b]Replacement[/b] — Swap one tile or wall type for another across an area. Substrate-variant aware (dirt ↔ grass, mud ↔ jungle grass). Tri-state filtering (tile only, wall only, both).

[*][b]Wiring[/b] — Place or remove red, green, blue, and yellow wire plus actuators in bulk. Full multiplayer support with server-side validation. Per-wire-type toggles.

[*][b]Safekeeping[/b] — Protect tiles from accidental modification. Persistent (saved with world) and session-only protection modes. Per-tile and per-wall protection.

[*][b]Coating[/b] — Paint tiles and walls (30+ colors), apply or remove illuminant and echo coatings, scrape moss, harvest hanging moss. Tri-state toggles. Color picker with swatch preview.

[*][b]Fluids[/b] — Pour water, lava, honey, or shimmer in shaped areas. Three fill modes: Full, Rain (with decorative rain cloud projectiles), and Pocket (sealed cavities). Drain with selective filtering. Bubble shell containment. Mix Liquids support (water+lava→obsidian, etc.) with particle effects. Achievement gates (planned).

[*][b]Torches[/b] — Place, replace, remove, or convert torches in geometric patterns. Biome-aware resolution (Torch God's Favor). Configurable grid spacing, Manhattan distance layouts, tiling alignment.

[*][b]Delimitation[/b] — Define a constraint area that filters all other wands. Canvas + selection system with boolean operations (Add, Remove, Intersect, XOR, Clear). 3-stencil slots with independent canvases. MagicWandRead integration for flood-fill canvas creation.

[*][b]Molding[/b] — Sculpt custom shapes by painting a mold. Auto-promotion to a reusable template for any other wand family. Canvas-based with boolean operations. Auto-Promote toggle for instant mold updates. Auto-Create Canvas and Auto-Expand Canvas toggles.

[*][b]Planification[/b] — Multi-stencil overlay planning system. 5 independent stencil slots, each with its own canvas + selection pair. Per-slot visibility and render config (outline, grid, fill). StencilTransform system for canvas+selection translation and pivot-based rotation/flip. Independent boolean operations per slot.
[/list]

[h2]🔥 Torch Wheel System[/h2]
Two standalone wands that automate torch placement along surfaces:
[list]
[*][b]Torch Wheel (Solid)[/b] — Follows solid walls, placing torches at configurable spacing with biome-aware resolution.
[*][b]Torch Wheel (Platform)[/b] — Traces platform paths, placing torches along platforms and handling falls and re-landing.
[*]Shift+right-click to kill all active torch wheel projectiles.
[/list]

[h2]🎮 4 Selection Modes[/h2]
Every wand comes in 4 variants — cycle by right-clicking in inventory:
[list]
[*][b]Instant[/b] — Click once, shape centered on cursor, executes immediately.
[*][b]Select[/b] — Click start corner, click end corner, executes.
[*][b]Confirm[/b] — Click start, click end, preview overlay, click to confirm or right-click to cancel.
[*][b]Stamp[/b] — Define a template, confirm, then click to repeat it anywhere.
[/list]

[h2]📐 Shape Types + MagicWandRead[/h2]
Rectangle (Filled/Hollow) · Ellipse (Filled/Hollow) · Diamond (Filled/Hollow) · Triangle (Filled/Hollow) · Half-Ellipse · Elbow (L-shape) · Cardinal Line · Straight Line · [b]Mold[/b] (custom shape) · [b]MagicWandRead[/b] (flood-fill selection)

Each shape supports [b]Filled[/b] and [b]Hollow[/b] modes with configurable outline thickness. [b]Slice Mode[/b] halves shapes in any direction. [b]Equal Dimensions[/b] forces square or circle proportions. [b]Connect Diameter[/b] fills between end points for Cardinal Lines.

[b]MagicWandRead[/b] — Flood-fill tool with 12 match types (SameTile, SameWall, Solid, Empty, Liquid, PaintTile, PaintWall, and more). 3 contiguity modes (4-way, 8-way, non-contiguous). Actuation filter. Safety gates block Instant/Select modes by default. Configurable 5000-tile cap. Results write to active canvas on Delimitation/Molding/Planification wands.

[h2]🎨 Stencil System[/h2]
[list]
[*]Multi-stencil slots (3 for Delimitation, 5 for Planification) with independent canvases and selections
[*]EDIT picker: choose which slot your active wand edits
[*]ACT-ON picker: choose which stencil the Mold cell stamps from — persists across all wands
[*]Persistence: canvases are saved with the world and synced in multiplayer
[/list]

[h2]🔄 Transform Mode[/h2]
[list]
[*]Move tool: drag canvas + selection to reposition
[*]Pivot system: set persistent or temporary rotation/flip anchor points
[*]Flip Horizontal / Flip Vertical
[*]Rotate 90° Clockwise / Counter-clockwise
[*]Auto-Create Canvas: automatically creates a canvas on first shape operation
[*]Auto-Expand Canvas: canvas auto-expands to include any shape tiles outside its boundary
[/list]

[h2]🎨 Visual Overlay System[/h2]
[list]

[*]Cancel overlay (red) on right-click cancellation
[*]Delimitation 3-layer overlay (canvas fill, outside dim, selection highlight) with gold border and transform indicators
[*]Molding canvas overlay with teal/cyan theme and transform indicators
[*]Planification overlay: renders all 5 stencil slots with per-slot color, outline/grid/fill
[*]Safekeeping overlay: cyan/magenta/gold indicators over protected tiles
[*]Torch tiling preview grid with BFS-computed positions
[*]Per-layer configurable colors and opacity for all overlays
[*]Fluid-aware overlay colors (water blue, lava orange, honey yellow, shimmer pink)
[/list]

Floating popout/panel sub-interfaces for deep configuration without leaving the main panel:
[list]
[*]Stencil EDIT picker — choose active stencil slot
[*]Stencil ACT-ON picker — choose Mold stamping stencil (persists across all wands)
[*]Color Replace Config — source/target paint + channel configuration
[*]MagicWandRead Config — sample mode + contiguity configuration
[*]Paint Color Popout — 32-swatch color grid
[*]Dismantling Options Popout — destroy toggles
[*]Transform Mode Shell — Move + Set Pivot buttons
[*]Lock/unlock and close behavior per SubUI
[/list]

[h2]📋 Inventory View[/h2]
Companion panel for explicit item selection:
[list]
[*]Per-source candidate slot grid for Building (explicit tile/wall type), Replacement (source/target), Torches (explicit torch type)
[*]Pin/Unpin system (right-click to pin, chosen overrides pinned)
[*]Ghost item substitution with rate-limited chat toast when chosen item is missing
[/list]

[h2]↩️ Full Undo/Redo[/h2]
20-deep undo stack. Every tile modification is recorded. Use [b]/undo[/b] and [b]/redo[/b] chat commands, or [b]/undoinfo[/b] to inspect the stack. Disabled by default (config toggle).

[h2]💬 Chat Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*]/wsw help — Display command help
[*]/wsw toolinfo — Toggle tooltip verbosity
[*]/wsw inventoryview — Toggle Inventory View panel
[*]/wsw overlay — Toggle overlay rendering
[*]/wsw paintdemo — Cycle paint demo overlay
[*]/paintdebug — Toggle paint debug overlay (dev)
[*]/shapetest — Debug shape rendering (dev)
[*]/undo / redo / undoinfo — Undo/redo stack
[/list]

[h2]🌍 Localization[/h2]
Full English localization. Locale files for German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (BR), Russian, and Chinese (Simplified) are included and will be completed for future updates.

[h2]🕸️ Multiplayer[/h2]
All operations use dedicated packet handlers per family. Client sends operation parameters to server; server validates and executes. 4-mode item cycling (not flags) for automatic MP sync. Efficient tile framing for large operations.

[h2]🔗 Cross-Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Thorium Mod: AnyGem, AnyGoldBar recipe groups
[*]Shimmer decrafting: all wands decraft to Amethyst
[*]Torch God's Favor: biome-aware torch resolution
[*]Paint Sprayer: can draw from Coating wand settings (cross-wand integration)
[/list]

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
Craft any wand at a Work Bench. Each family has its own recipe using gems and bars. Right-click in inventory to cycle selection modes. Open the settings panel to configure shape, mode, and toggles.
