[h1]World Shaping Wands[/h1]

[b]46 wands across 11 families[/b] for large-scale world editing, plus 2 Torch Wheel wands. Build, shape, paint, wire, flood, torch, constrain, plan, and mold any region using geometric shapes.

[h2]🔨 11 Wand Families[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Building[/b] — Place tiles, walls, platforms, rope, tracks, planter boxes, and grass seeds in bulk. Slope/HalfBlock modes (6 types + actuation). Block exhaustion (NextBlock/Interrupt/Cancel). Paint Sprayer integration. Vacuum collection. Progressive batching.

[*][b]Dismantling[/b] — Mass-destroy tiles and walls. Pickaxe validation. Container destruction with auto-unlock. Void Everything mode. Delicate tile and demon altar protection.

[*][b]Replacement[/b] — Swap tile/wall types across an area. Substrate-variant aware (dirt↔grass, mud↔jungle grass). Tri-state filtering.

[*][b]Wiring[/b] — Place/remove red, green, blue, and yellow wire plus actuators. Full multiplayer with server-side validation.

[*][b]Safekeeping[/b] — Protect tiles from modification. Persistent (world-saved) and session-only modes.

[*][b]Coating[/b] — Paint tiles/walls (30+ colors). Illuminant/echo coatings. Moss scrape/harvest. Tri-state toggles. Color swatch picker.

[*][b]Fluids[/b] — Pour water, lava, honey, or shimmer. Three fill modes (Full, Rain with cloud effects, Pocket). Selective drain. Bubble shell. Mix Liquids (obsidian, honeyblock, crispy honey).

[*][b]Torches[/b] — Place/replace/remove/convert torches. Biome-aware resolution (Torch God's Favor). Grid spacing, Manhattan distance, tiling alignment.

[*][b]Delimitation[/b] — Constraint area filtering all other wands. Canvas + selection with boolean ops (Add/Remove/Intersect/XOR). 3 stencil slots. MagicWandRead flood-fill integration.

[*][b]Molding[/b] — Sculpt custom shapes by painting a mold. Auto-promotes to reusable template. Boolean ops. Auto-Create/Expand Canvas toggles.

[*][b]Planification[/b] — Multi-stencil planning. 5 independent slots with per-slot canvas, selection, visibility, and render config (outline/grid/fill). Transform system for moves and pivots.
[/list]

[h2]🔥 Torch Wheel System[/h2]
Two standalone wands: [b]Torch Wheel (Solid)[/b] follows walls placing torches at configurable spacing. [b]Torch Wheel (Platform)[/b] traces platform paths. Shift+right-click kills all active projectiles.

[h2]🎮 4 Selection Modes (per wand)[/h2]
Right-click in inventory to cycle: [b]Instant[/b] (click once, centered) · [b]Select[/b] (click two corners) · [b]Confirm[/b] (preview then confirm/cancel) · [b]Stamp[/b] (define template, repeat anywhere).

[h2]📐 Shape Types + MagicWandRead[/h2]
Rectangle · Ellipse · Diamond · Triangle · Half-Ellipse · Elbow (L-shape) · Cardinal Line · Straight Line · [b]Mold[/b] (custom) · [b]MagicWandRead[/b] (flood-fill)

Each supports Filled/Hollow with outline thickness, Slice Mode, Equal Dimensions, and Invert Selection. [b]MagicWandRead[/b] floods with 12 match types, 3 contiguity modes, actuation filter, safety gates, and 5000-tile cap. Results write to active canvas.

[h2]🎨 Stencil System[/h2]
Multi-slot canvases (3 Delimitation, 5 Planification) with independent selections. EDIT picker chooses active slot; ACT-ON picker chooses Mold stamping stencil — persists across all wands. Canvases save with world and sync in multiplayer.

[h2]🔄 Transform Mode[/h2]
Move canvas+selection. Set persistent/temporary pivot anchors. Flip Horizontal/Vertical. Rotate 90° CW/CCW. Auto-Create Canvas and Auto-Expand Canvas toggles.

[h2]🎨 7 Overlays[/h2]
Shape preview with dimension labels and tile count · Cancel (red) · Delimitation 3-layer with gold border · Molding teal canvas · Planification per-slot colors · Safekeeping protection indicators · Torch tiling preview grid. All colors/opacity configurable.

[h2]⚙️ Configuration[/h2]
11 per-wand settings panels with draggable icon-based UI. 11 config files (Resources, Sandbox, Limits, Performance, Stamp, TorchWheel, Carefree, Overlay, Canvas Overlay, Preferences, MagicWandReadSafety). Infinite resources per type. Tooltip verbosity toggle. Panel drag mode.

[h2]📦 SubUI Popout System[/h2]
7 floating sub-interfaces: Stencil EDIT/ACT-ON pickers, Color Replace Config, MagicWandRead Config, Paint Color Popout (32 swatches), Dismantling Options Popout, Transform Mode Shell. Lock/unlock and close behavior.

[h2]📋 Inventory View[/h2]
Companion panel for explicit item selection (Building, Replacement, Torches). Pin/Unpin system. Ghost item substitution with chat toast.

[h2]↩️ Undo/Redo[/h2]
20-deep undo stack. /undo, /redo, /undoinfo commands. Disabled by default.

[h2]💬 Commands[/h2]
/wsw help · /wsw toolinfo · /wsw inventoryview · /wsw overlay · /undo /redo /undoinfo

[h2]🌍 Localization[/h2]
Full English. Locale files for German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (BR), Russian, Chinese (Simplified) included for future completion.

[h2]🕸️ Multiplayer[/h2]
Dedicated packet handlers per family. Server-side validation. 4-mode item cycling for automatic sync. Efficient tile framing.

[h2]🔗 Cross-Compatibility[/h2]
Thorium Mod gems · Shimmer decraft (→ Amethyst) · Torch God's Favor · Cross-wand Paint Sprayer integration.

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
Craft any wand at a Work Bench (gems + bars). Right-click in inventory to cycle modes. Open the settings panel to configure shape and options.
