World Shaping Wands — 46 wands across 11 families for large-scale world
editing, plus 2 Torch Wheel wands. Build, shape, paint, wire, flood, torch,
constrain, plan, and mold any region using geometric shapes.

11 Wand Families:
  • Building — Place tiles, walls, platforms, rope, planter boxes, tracks,
    and grass seeds in bulk. Slope/HalfBlock (6 slope types + actuation).
    Block exhaustion modes (NextBlock/Interrupt/Cancel). Paint Sprayer
    integration. Vacuum mode. Progressive batching.
  • Dismantling — Mass-destroy tiles and walls. Pickaxe validation.
    Container destruction with auto-unlock. Void Everything. Delicate tile
    protection. Demon altar handling.
  • Replacement — Swap tile/wall types across an area. Substrate-variant
    aware (dirt<->grass, mud<->jungle grass). Tri-state filtering.
  • Wiring — Place/remove wire colors and actuators in bulk. Full
    multiplayer with server-side validation. Per-wire toggles.
  • Safekeeping — Protect tiles from accidental modification. Persistent
    (world-saved) and session-only modes. Per-tile/per-wall protection.
  • Coating — Paint tiles/walls (30+ colors). Illuminant/echo coatings.
    Moss scrape/harvest. Tri-state toggles. Color picker swatches.
  • Fluids — Pour water, lava, honey, shimmer. Three fill modes (Full,
    Rain with cloud effects, Pocket). Selective drain. Bubble shell.
    Mix Liquids support with particle effects.
  • Torches — Place/replace/remove/convert torches in patterns. Biome-aware
    resolution. Configurable grid spacing and alignment.
  • Delimitation — Constraint area filtering all other wands. Canvas +
    selection with boolean ops (Add/Remove/Intersect/XOR/Clear). 3-stencil
    slots. MagicWandRead flood-fill integration.
  • Molding — Sculpt custom shapes by painting a mold. Auto-promotion to
    reusable template. Boolean ops. Auto-Create/Expand Canvas toggles.
  • Planification — Multi-stencil overlay planning. 5 independent slots
    with per-slot canvases and selections. Per-slot visibility/render
    config (outline/grid/fill). Transform system for moves and pivots.

Torch Wheel System:
  • Torch Wheel (Solid) — Follows walls, placing torches at configurable
    spacing with biome-aware resolution.
  • Torch Wheel (Platform) — Traces platform paths.
  • Shift+right-click to kill all active projectiles.

Selection Modes (each wand has all four):
  Instant — Click once, shape centered on cursor, executes immediately.
  Select  — Click start corner, click end corner, executes.
  Confirm — Click start, click end, preview overlay, confirm or cancel.
  Stamp   — Define a template, confirm, click to repeat anywhere.

Shape Types + MagicWandRead:
  Rectangle, Ellipse, Diamond, Triangle, Half-Ellipse, Elbow (L-shape),
  Cardinal Line, Straight Line, Mold (custom), MagicWandRead (flood-fill).
  All support Filled/Hollow with outline thickness, slice mode, equal
  dimensions, and invert selection. MagicWandRead has 12 match types,
  3 contiguity modes, safety gates, and 5000-tile cap.
